How a cleaning decision was checked

No Codex suggestion was used to choose the cleaning rules. To independently verify the currency-conversion decision, I selected the original Australia coffee retail-value row for 2025 (AUD 1,389.8 million), looked up the pinned World Bank annual-average rate (1.55199789 AUD per USD), calculated 1,389.8 ÷ 1.55199789 = 895.490908 USD million, and compared it with the cleaned CSV. Limitations

  • Annual-average exchange rates do not adjust for inflation, purchasing power, or within-year currency changes.
  • Interpolated values are estimates, not source observations. Linear fallback is used where cubic spline interpolation is unsupported or invalid.
  • Rows with no annual observations are excluded; the workflow does not impute missing key fields.
  • Metric tonnes, liters, pack units, and channel percentages are distinct dimensions and should not be directly combined.
  • These data support descriptive comparisons, not causal claims or proof that the market estimates are representative.
