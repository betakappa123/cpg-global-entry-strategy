2. Explanation of How One Codex / AI Suggestion Was Checked

Context: During exploratory automated script generation, an AI coding assistant (Codex/LLM) suggested a blanket imputation snippet: df = df.replace('-', 0).infer_objects(copy=False) to streamline numeric conversion and avoid handling missing values.

Audit & Verification Applied: We inspected which exact rows in each dataset contained hyphens. In Market Sizes.xls, hyphens appeared exclusively in India RTD Coffee Off-trade Volume and Off-trade Value RSP across all 11 years (2015–2025). Consulting Euromonitor's research methodologies revealed that hyphens signify unobserved or untracked market segments (Euromonitor did not measure RTD liquid coffee in India during this horizon), not zero consumption.

Downstream Mathematical Fallout: Imputing India RTD volume as 0.0 would falsely imply zero consumption, cause divide-by-zero errors in CAGR calculations, and artificially dilute Asia-Pacific per-capita benchmarks across India's 1.4 billion population.

Decision Made: We rejected the uniform zero-imputation rule. In Retail channels, where hyphens represent inactive/unmeasured channels (e.g. forecourt retailers in China), values are treated as 0.0%; in Market Sizes, hyphens are preserved strictly as NaN so unobserved market segments are excluded from aggregates without corrupting averages.
